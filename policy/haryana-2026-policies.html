Haryana Progressive MSME & Export Promotion Policy 2026 and Linked Agri-Processing Schemes

Research note for MSME and farm-proceeds planning in the Pehowa–Kurukshetra–Jhajjar area. Compiled 21 November 2026. Figures drawn from gazette notifications and contemporaneous official summaries. Verify on the single-window portal before application.

What “16 November 2026 scheme notifications” means

The parent Haryana Progressive MSME & Export Promotion Policy 2026 was notified on 29 July 2026 (Gazette Extraordinary No. 114-2026/Ext., notification 02/04/2026-1IB-II). On 16 November 2026 the Directorate of MSME issued a series of operational scheme notifications in Gazette Extraordinary No. 175-2026/Ext. These turn the policy’s high-level promises into claimable schemes with application forms, eligibility rules and reimbursement procedures. Parallel notifications under the Agribusiness and Food Processing Policy 2026 were issued around the same date.

1. Parent policies

Haryana Progressive MSME & Export Promotion Policy 2026

  • Notified 29 July 2026; operative for five years.
  • Aims: attract >₹55,000 crore manufacturing investment, create >5 lakh jobs, double exports.
  • 60 initiatives across finance, infrastructure, technology, exports, sustainability and skills.
  • Creates a dedicated MSME Directorate and strengthens the single-window system.
  • Thrust sectors include agri-based, food processing & allied industry (with a separate dedicated policy).

Primary source: Haryana Government Gazette Extraordinary, 29 July 2026. Secondary summaries: CMO Haryana press material, Times of India, The Hindu.

Haryana Agribusiness and Food Processing Policy 2026

  • Notified earlier in 2026 (around May); operational schemes published mid-November 2026.
  • Aims: attract ₹5,000 crore private investment, create ~30,000 jobs, raise food-processing exports by 50 %, reduce post-harvest losses.
  • Focuses on value addition, cold chains, packaging, logistics and farm-to-market linkages.

2. Key operational schemes relevant to processing, cold chain and digital tools

2.1 Capital Investment Subsidy (Haryana Udyam Vikas Mission)

For thrust-sector manufacturing MSMEs and exporters (new / expansion / diversification / modernisation).

AreaSubsidy rateMaximum
Core15 %₹2 crore
Intermediate20 %₹2.5 crore
Sub-Prime25 %₹3.5 crore
Prime / Focus30 %₹4 crore

Agri-based / food processing is a listed thrust sector. Extra 5 % for women, SC, Divyang, transgender, ex-servicemen or Agniveer promoters (cap unchanged). Notification 16 November 2026 under the MSME policy.

2.2 Adoption of AI Solution / Labs Scheme

  • For registered manufacturing micro and small enterprises.
  • 33 % reimbursement of eligible expenditure:
  • Up to ₹10 lakh for standalone or subscription-based AI solutions (subscriptions eligible up to three years).
  • Up to ₹15 lakh for an in-house AI laboratory (computing, GPUs, specialised hardware, software licences required for AI work).
  • Once during the policy period.
  • Must be procured from Haryana-based technology providers or developed internally. Conventional software and routine maintenance excluded.
  • Eligibility conditions include Udyam registration, ZED Bronze certification, regular operation, no wilful default / blacklisting.
  • Encouraged uses: process automation, predictive maintenance, quality control, demand forecasting, data-driven decision-making.

Notified 16 November 2026 (Notification 02/04/2026-1IB-II).

3. Relevance to Pehowa–Kurukshetra–Jhajjar MSME / farm-proceeds planning

Processing, cold chain and digital tools reduce post-harvest losses, raise value addition, improve export readiness and can be claimed under the schemes above. Area classification (Core / Intermediate / Sub-Prime / Prime) determines the exact rate and cap. Confirm the classification of the specific site on the Invest Haryana / MSME Directorate portal.

Illustrative project ideas

  • Oilseed crushing and refining — expeller + refining line; MSME capital subsidy or 25 % agri-processing support; cake sold as feed.
  • Millet (bajra/jowar) milling and ready mixes — low-water, circular-economy angle; AI vision sorter for quality.
  • Vegetable puree / paste line — pulper, concentrator, aseptic filler; cold-chain elements for pulp.
  • Village pack house + pre-cooling — grading and pre-cooling for local horticulture; cold-chain or linkages scheme.
  • Multi-temperature cold store + reefer — shared or proprietary; serves multiple small processors.
  • AI quality-sorting and predictive maintenance — computer vision on sorting line; sensor-based maintenance on dryers/boilers; 33 % AI reimbursement if ZED Bronze is held.
  • Residue briquetting or bio-energy — circular-economy thrust under MSME capital subsidy.

4. Practical notes

  • Applications run through the Haryana Single Window System (SWS) portal.
  • Cumulative incentives under the MSME policy plus central schemes are capped at 100 % of fixed capital investment in some schemes.
  • AI support requires ZED Bronze and is limited to micro/small manufacturing units.
  • Exact stacking of MSME capital subsidy and agri-policy subsidies needs confirmation against the full gazette text for each project.
  • Statutory clearances (FSSAI, HSPCB, etc.) remain mandatory.
