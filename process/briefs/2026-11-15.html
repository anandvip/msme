07NCDs (non-convertible debentures)

⚠ Risk box — read before considering any NCD

  • Lower investment grade: BBB+ / BB+ ratings carry material credit and default risk, especially for NBFCs and MFIs.
  • No DICGC cover: unlike bank FDs, there is no deposit insurance.
  • Thin liquidity: secondary-market selling can be slow or at a discount.
  • Security varies: "secured" does not guarantee full recovery; asset cover differs by issue.
  • Capital is returned at maturity only if the issuer performs.
  • Not as safe as bank FDs or G-Secs. Diversify and size positions carefully.
  • Yields here are indicative only (T2–T3 quotes), not offers.
  • Exact matches for short-term secured NCDs with YTM >12% in the BB+/BBB band are limited. flag
  • Closest primary issue: Muthoot Mercantile NCD — open 12–26 Nov; secured; rated ACUITE A; highest effective yield ~11.57%; tenors 13–78 months. T2 flag (rated A, i.e. above the BBB/BB+ band asked for, and yield below 12%)
  • Secondary market: BBB+ secured NCDs at YTM ~11.75–11.80%, monthly/quarterly payouts (Finnable / Mahaveer-type), 28–34 months residual. T2–T3, indicative
  • Coupon is usually lower than YTM when the bond trades at a discount.

Rice

  • Non-basmati white rice exports banned 20 July. Eased in September: MEP removed on basmati; non-basmati white allowed above $490/t; parboiled duty cut 20% → 10%. revised
  • ~150,000 t of cargo trapped at ports cleared after the duty change.
  • NCE (as reported; likely NCEL, National Cooperative Exports Ltd) exports: 900,000 t broken rice (Senegal 500k, Indonesia 200k, Mali 100k, Gambia 50k) + 35,000 t wheat/wheat products incl. Bhutan.
  • Wholesale prices +7% after the US–Iran ceasefire set off a Middle East buying rush.
  • 400,000 t basmati stranded during the conflict; ₹2–3k crore payments stuck (mostly Iran).
  • 5% broken parboiled hit a 5-year high of ~$409–416/t on thin paddy; other weeks near 2-year lows of $377–391 as African demand moderated at higher prices.
  • Agri exports +8% to $14B (Apr–Nov); rice +13% to $7.3B in the first 8 months.
  • Centre ordered full rice/paddy stock disclosure on 6 Nov; stock limits in 5 southern states through 31 Jan 2027.
  • OMSS rice e-auction target doubled to 5 Mt.
  • At the WTO, the US/UK/EU/Canada/Australia are questioning India's wheat/rice/onion curbs. Commerce Minister Goyal: no plan to lift.
  • Rice production forecast trimmed to 147 Mt (weak monsoon) vs 154 Mt last year (−4.5% derived); exports still seen at 23–25 Mt. revised
